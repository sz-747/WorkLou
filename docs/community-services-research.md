# Real community services research — Lose Place referral categories

Researched 5 September 2026 via web search against official organisation websites, NSW Government
pages, and community directories. This document replaces the invented "mock" services (Redfern
Women's Refuge, Sanctuary House, etc.) with **real services** that Lose Place would realistically
refer women to, matching the eight categories our seed data uses. Where the current mock seed has
no real equivalent in that exact suburb, the mapping table at the end notes the best real option.

> **Before seeding:** confirm hours and intake rules by phone — refuges keep addresses confidential
> and intake is usually via a central line, not walk-up. Phone numbers below are as published by
> each organisation.

---

## Crisis lines (always list first — 24/7)

### NSW Domestic Violence Line — 1800 65 64 63
- **Who:** women (and others) experiencing domestic violence in NSW, 24/7, free, interpreter available.
- **What:** crisis counselling, safety planning, and referral to refuges, crisis accommodation and
  DFV services statewide. This is the main entry point for refuge placements.

### 1800RESPECT — 1800 737 732
- **Who:** anyone affected by sexual, domestic or family violence, Australia-wide, 24/7.
- **What:** national counselling, information and referral service.

### Link2home — 1800 152 152
- **Who:** anyone homeless or at risk of homelessness in NSW, 24/7 (NSW Government).
- **What:** entry point for emergency and crisis accommodation, including women fleeing DFV.

## 1. Emergency accommodation / refuges

### WAGEC — Women's & Girls' Emergency Centre (Redfern)
- **Who:** women and children impacted by homelessness, domestic violence and social disadvantage
  across inner Sydney.
- **What:** specialist homelessness service — shelter for around 200 women and children a night,
  emergency accommodation, material aid, safety planning, case management and bio-psychosocial
  support. Redfern head office offers both **appointments and drop-in**.
- **Web:** wagec.org.au

### Elsie Women's Refuge (Glebe)
- **Who:** women 18+ with children who have experienced domestic or family violence. Australia's
  first women's refuge (opened 1974).
- **What:** crisis refuge accommodation; office contact hours Mon–Fri 9am–4pm. Intake is via the
  Domestic Violence Line, not direct.
- **Note:** addresses of refuges are confidential — record the intake line, not a street address.

### Parramatta Women's Shelter
- **Who:** women and their children experiencing domestic and family abuse, western Sydney.
- **What:** crisis accommodation and outreach support.
- **Web:** parrawomensshelter.org.au

### The Sanctuary — The Hills Women's Shelter (Castle Hill)
- **Who:** women and their children escaping homelessness and/or domestic and family violence.
- **What:** safe, temporary, supported shelter for up to 3 months, with around-the-clock support.
- **Note:** our mock seed placed a "Sanctuary House" in Surry Hills — the real Sanctuary is in The
  Hills. For inner-city crisis beds, WAGEC or Link2home is the appropriate referral.

## 2. DFV specialist support

### Bankstown Domestic Violence Service (BDVS) — Justice Support Centre
- **Who:** women and children impacted by DFV in Canterbury-Bankstown; explicitly inclusive of
  trans women and sistergirls.
- **What:** specialist DFV case management, safety planning, information and referral.
- **Phone:** 9790 1380 · **Web:** jsc.org.au

### Sydney WDVCAS (Sydney Women's Domestic Violence Court Advocacy Service)
- **Who:** women and children in the Sydney area going through the courts for protection.
- **What:** free court advocacy — help obtaining Apprehended Domestic Violence Orders (ADVOs),
  safety planning, support at court, liaison with police. Operated by **Redfern Legal Centre**;
  part of the statewide Legal Aid NSW WDVCAP program (136 local courts).
- **Web:** rlc.org.au · legalaid.nsw.gov.au

### NSW Domestic Violence Line (see crisis lines above)
The real "Crisis Support Line — DFV" from our mock data.

### DV West (Blacktown / Hills)
- **Who:** women and children experiencing DFV in Blacktown and The Hills.
- **What:** specialist case management, crisis refuge accommodation and transitional housing.
- **Web:** dvwest.org.au

## 3. Staying Home Leaving Violence (replaces mock "Staying Home Safely")

NSW Department of Communities and Justice program operating in 43+ locations statewide.
- **Who:** women and children who want to **stay in their own home** after ending a violent
  relationship.
- **What:** works with Police to remove the violent partner from the home, then provides security
  upgrades, case management and support so the woman and children remain safely housed — a
  homelessness-prevention program.
- **Web:** dcj.nsw.gov.au (search "Staying Home Leaving Violence" for program locations)

## 4. Transitional housing

### Women's Housing Company
- **Who:** women with a particular focus on older women, women with children, and women who have
  experienced domestic and family violence.
- **What:** crisis and temporary accommodation, transitional housing, and homelessness support
  across Sydney.
- **Web:** womenshousingcompany.org.au

## 5. Food assistance

### Addi Road Food Pantry (Marrickville)
- **Who:** anyone in need — free emergency food relief, no questions asked. Feeds ~9,000 people a
  week through its Marrickville and Camperdown pantries.
- **What:** free/affordable grocery pantry and free hot meals.
  Address: 142 Addison Road, Marrickville. Mon–Fri 12–4pm, Wed until 7pm (hot meals Wednesday).
- **Web:** addiroad.org.au

### OzHarvest Market Waterloo
- **Who:** anyone in need — **no ID or card required**.
- **What:** free "take what you need, give if you can" rescued-food market, Tue–Sat.
- **Web:** ozharvest.org (Market Waterloo)

### OzHarvest Refettorio (Surry Hills)
- **Who:** anyone in need.
- **What:** free restaurant-quality rescued meals, no charge. 481 Crown Street, Surry Hills.
  Tue–Fri 12–1:30pm, Sat 9:30–11:30am.

### City of Sydney Food Support Directory
- Ongoing, Council-maintained directory of free meals and food services in Redfern, Waterloo and
  the wider City of Sydney — useful when a client needs something near a specific location.
- **Web:** cityofsydney.nsw.gov.au/public-health-safety-programs/food-support-directory

## 6. Legal assistance

### Redfern Legal Centre (Redfern)
- **Who:** people in Sydney who can't afford a lawyer; specialist practice in issues affecting women.
- **What:** free legal advice, referral and casework. Specialist areas: **domestic violence,
  financial abuse, tenancy, credit and debt**. Runs the Domestic Violence Legal Advice Line:
  02 8745 6999 / 1800 810 784. Also hosts the inner-Sydney Tenants' Advice and Advocacy Service
  and Sydney WDVCAS.
- **Web:** rlc.org.au

### Women's Legal Service NSW (WLSNSW)
- **Who:** women across NSW. Free, confidential, women-only legal service.
- **What:** advice, information, referral and casework on **family law, parenting issues, domestic
  violence and sexual assault**; help accessing Centrelink and Victims Services. Advice line
  02 8745 6988 / 1800 810 784 (set session times — Mon 9:30–12:30, Tue 1:30–4:30). Also runs
  **Willow**, a free online DFV legal information service.
- **Web:** wlsnsw.org.au · willow.org.au

### Welfare Rights Centre (Surry Hills)
- **Who:** NSW residents adversely affected by Centrelink decisions — 40+ years' specialist practice.
- **What:** free legal advice, assistance and representation for Centrelink problems (refused
  claims, debts, appeals). Phone advice 10am–4pm Mon–Fri.
- **Web:** welfarerightscentre.org.au

### Macarthur Legal Centre (Campbelltown)
- **Who:** people in the Macarthur region who can't afford a lawyer.
- **What:** free legal advice, referrals and casework including **domestic and family violence and
  financial matters**. 02 4628 2042, Mon/Tue/Thu 9am–4:30pm.
- **Web:** maclegal.net.au

### Tenants' Advice and Advocacy Services (Tenants' Union NSW network)
- **Who:** renters across NSW, including women leaving violence (see Tenants' Union factsheet
  "Domestic violence and renting" — tenants' rights to end leases early after DV).
- **What:** free tenancy advice and advocacy; the inner-Sydney service is hosted by Redfern Legal
  Centre.
- **Web:** tenants.org.au

## 7. Health services

### Bankstown Women's Health Centre (Bankstown)
- **Who:** women, female-identifying people and carers living in Bankstown and nearby suburbs.
- **What:** free or discounted health and wellbeing services — generalist counselling, child
  sexual assault counselling (women, teens, boys and girls), bulk-billed medical services,
  dietetics, social work, case management, therapeutic supports, food-share program and health
  promotion. Trauma-informed. One of 22 centres in the Women's Health NSW network.
- **Address:** 4–26 Jacobs St, Bankstown · **Web:** bwhc.org.au

### Leichhardt Women's Community Health Centre (Leichhardt — serves inner west incl. Redfern area)
- **Who:** women in Sydney's inner west.
- **What:** low-cost medical and allied health, women's health GP clinics, nurse clinics, counselling,
  naturopathy and health education.
- **Phone:** 02 9556 3011 · **Web:** lwchc.org.au

### Sydney Local Health District — Community Health / Women's Health Program (incl. Newtown centre)
- **Who:** residents of the Sydney Local Health District (Redfern, Waterloo, Newtown, Glebe, etc.),
  prioritising women from priority populations.
- **What:** public community health — nursing, medical and allied health close to home, plus a
  district Women's Health Program.
- **Web:** slhd.health.nsw.gov.au

## 8. Centrelink & financial support

### Welfare Rights Centre (see Legal above) — the real "Centrelink help" referral
Free advice/representation for Centrelink problems, not a Centrelink office.

### Wesley Mission — Financial Counselling
- **Who:** anyone in financial difficulty in NSW.
- **What:** free, confidential financial counselling — debt, bills, hardship negotiation.
- **Phone:** 1300 827 638 · **Web:** wesleymission.org.au

### Salvos Moneycare (The Salvation Army)
- **Who:** anyone facing or wanting to avoid financial difficulty.
- **What:** free financial counselling and support.
- **Web:** salvationarmy.org.au/moneycare

### Salvos Doorways — crisis relief
- **Who:** people in crisis.
- **What:** emergency relief — food vouchers and parcels, financial aid, utility/rent help.
- **Web:** salvationarmy.org.au (Crisis relief & Doorways)

### Western Sydney MRC — Emergency Relief
- **Who:** individuals and families in South-Western and Outer South-Western Sydney experiencing
  financial hardship (strong multicultural focus).
- **What:** emergency relief covering food, housing and healthcare costs, plus casework.
- **Web:** wsmrc.org.au

### Known data caveats for seeding
1. **Refuge addresses are confidential** — store the intake line (DV Line / Link2home), not an address.
2. Several services are **phone-advice only** with set session times (WLSNSW, Welfare Rights,
   Macarthur Legal) — set `walk_in_allowed = false`, `appointment_required = true`.
3. WAGEC Redfern office and BDVS accept drop-ins/appointments; OzHarvest and Addi Road are
   walk-in with no ID.
4. Children policies: refuges and WAGEC shelter **children**; the WDVCAS, legal lines and pantries
   serve women with children too. Confirm per service before setting booleans.
